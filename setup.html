Worn equipment is weaker: each slot is narrowed to items that actually go in it, so the answer is always a real helmet or a real ring, but often the wrong one, and the right answer is frequently the second suggestion. Slots the matcher is unsure about are outlined; click any slot to pick from the alternatives or search by name.
